## Ethical principles

### Beneficence
- Intent to do good by helping promote growth in others.

### Nonmaleficence
- Doing no harm to others.
- Helpers should make sure their actions do not inadvertently harm those they help.

### Justice
- Fairness or ensuring equality of opportunities and resources for all people.

**Examples**
- Not-for-profit care
- Shelters
- Free clinics

## Fidelity
- Keeping promises and trust between people.
- Crucial component.
- Important to building therapeutic relationship.
- Example: be on time, not following through on promises.

## Veracity
- Telling the truth.
- Giving honest but tactful feedback, which can be done more as therapeutic relationship grows.

## Autonomy
- Refers to choices a client can make based on their own belief system.
