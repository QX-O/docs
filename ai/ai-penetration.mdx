## Top 10 Activities With Highest AI Penetration

AI usage among those who routinely engage in a specific activity.

1. **51% — Writing support**: Essay editing, creative writing assistance.
2. **47% — Coding projects**: Building apps for work, school, or personal use.
3. **43% — Getting help on assignments**: Homework, work projects.
4. **38% — Creating presentations**: Work slides, pitch decks, educational presentations.
5. **37% — Creating music + audio**: Song composition, podcast production, audio editing.
6. **36%**\* — Research for work or school: Industry trends, report topics.
7. **34% — Creating images**: Digital art, social media graphics, illustrations.
8. **34%**\*\* — Managing childcare: Caregiver coordination, scheduling and logistics.
9. **31% — Dating or social coaching**: Dating apps, matchmaking, relationship advice.
10. **30% — Learning a new language**: Language lessons, conversation practice.


## Top 10 Activities With Lowest AI Penetration

AI usage among those who routinely engage in a specific activity.

1. **16% — Navigating healthcare**: Dealing with healthcare, insurance, caregivers.
2. **18% — Staying in touch**: Using technology to keep up with friends, family, etc.
3. **18% — Planning workouts**: Fitness programs, workout schedules, exercise tips.
4. **18% — Managing expenses**: Monthly bill payments, expense tracking, budgeting.
5. **20% — Learning a new sport**: Sports techniques, form correction, game strategies.
6. **20% — Researching health questions**: Symptoms, treatments.
7. **20% — Home repairs + maintenance**: DIY projects, design tools.
8. **21% — Mental health and wellness support**: Therapy, guided meditation.
9. **22% — Tracking health and fitness data**: Monitoring vitals and sleep.
10. **22% — Meal planning**: Weekly meal prep, grocery lists, recipes.

## Notes

- \*Asked only to students and the employed.
- \*\*Asked only to parents of children under 18.
