# Authority-Bible-XOQ

Authority-Bible-XOQ is a spiritual teaching and study space created to help readers grow in biblical understanding, explore biblical artifacts, and deepen their knowledge of God, the Old and New Testament, and most of all Jesus Christ.

## What this site is for

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Biblical understanding" icon="book-bible">
    Follow guided lessons and tutorials that build familiarity with Scripture, biblical context, and faithful interpretation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Artifact studies" icon="landmark">
    Explore biblical artifacts and historical materials that can support deeper study and reflection.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Old and New Testament learning" icon="scroll">
    Review themes, people, places, and teachings across both Testaments with an emphasis on clarity and growth.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Following Jesus Christ" icon="cross">
    Keep the life, teachings, sacrifice, and resurrection of Jesus Christ at the center of spiritual learning.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Mission

This documentation exists as a place to showcase teaching materials for others to follow, review, and use as they gain their own understanding of Scripture and faith.
