# Ideas & Scripture Notebook

Welcome. This site is a Mintlify-powered knowledge base that you can use to collect ideas, Bible verses, study notes, reflections, and reusable writing templates.

<Note>
  This repo started as the Mintlify starter kit. The most important files for your personal notebook are `docs.json`, `index.mdx`, `ideas/index.mdx`, `scripture/index.mdx`, and the templates in `templates/`.
</Note>

## Is this a good fit for your goal?

Yes, this is a good foundation if you want your ideas and Bible notes to become a clean, searchable website. It is better than a loose notes folder when you want structure, navigation, reusable templates, and a polished reading experience.

It may not be the best fit if you want a private daily journal, encrypted notes, or a fast mobile-first capture app. In that case, use this repo as the polished archive and keep raw private notes somewhere else.

## Recommended next changes

1. Rename the site in `docs.json` once you choose a name.
2. Replace the starter logo and favicon.
3. Remove the `api-reference/` folder if you do not plan to document an API.
4. Add your first real note under `ideas/` or `scripture/`.
5. Keep the templates and update them as your style develops.
