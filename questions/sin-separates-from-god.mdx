## The extracted question

The notes state that sin separates you from God.

## Short answer

Sin separates by breaking fellowship, hardening the heart, distorting desire, and placing guilt between the sinner and a holy God. In Christ, separation is answered by confession, repentance, forgiveness, and reconciliation.

## What separation means

Separation does not mean God becomes unaware of a person. God sees all things. It means sin damages communion with God and places a person in need of mercy, cleansing, and restored fellowship.

## How sin separates

### Sin creates guilt

Guilt is not only a feeling. It is a real moral problem before God. The gospel does not merely soothe guilt; it deals with guilt through Christ.

### Sin hardens the heart

Repeated disobedience can make a person less sensitive to conviction. What once felt alarming can start to feel normal.

### Sin distorts desire

Sin trains appetite away from God. Over time, the person may not only commit sin but also begin to defend it.

### Sin damages relationships

Sin rarely stays private. It affects trust, speech, family, friendship, community, and witness.

## The difference between conviction and condemnation

- **Conviction** names sin so you can return to God.
- **Condemnation** names sin as if there is no way back.

The Holy Spirit convicts with truth and hope. The enemy accuses with shame and despair.

## The way back

1. Name the sin honestly.
2. Confess it to God.
3. Receive Christ’s mercy.
4. Turn from the pattern.
5. Seek accountability where needed.
6. Repair what can be repaired.
