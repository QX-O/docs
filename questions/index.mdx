## Suggested path

1. Begin with [Jesus without sin](/questions/jesus-without-sin), because it establishes Christ as the faithful Son and perfect sacrifice.
2. Continue to [the death of Christ](/questions/satan-delay-death-of-christ), because the cross explains why Satan’s apparent victory became his defeat.
3. Move through [pride](/questions/pride-before-fall), [sin](/questions/sin-separates-from-god), and [anger](/questions/anger-foothold) to see common access points for deception.
4. Study [dreams](/questions/satan-use-dreams) and [warnings](/questions/god-warns-where-satan-wars) together, because both require careful discernment rather than fear.
5. Finish with [the law of knowing](/questions/law-of-knowing) and [good works with posture](/questions/good-works-and-posture), because revelation should lead to humble obedience.
