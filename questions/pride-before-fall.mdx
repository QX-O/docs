## The extracted question

The notes state that pride comes before a fall because pride is blinding.

## Short answer

Pride comes before a fall because it makes people overestimate themselves, underestimate danger, resist correction, and ignore dependence on God.

## What pride does

### Pride blinds you to weakness

A proud person may see temptation as something that defeats other people, not them. That false confidence reduces watchfulness.

### Pride rejects correction

Correction feels like humiliation when pride is in control. Instead of receiving warning as mercy, pride treats warning as insult.

### Pride distorts spiritual perception

Pride can make a person mistake stubbornness for strength, ambition for calling, and self-protection for wisdom.

### Pride isolates

Because pride resists accountability, it pulls a person away from the voices that could help them recover before collapse.

## The biblical pattern

Scripture repeatedly connects pride with downfall and humility with grace. The pattern is not mechanical superstition; it is moral reality. A proud person walks without the guardrails of dependence, repentance, and counsel.

## How humility protects you

Humility says:

- I can be wrong.
- I need God’s mercy.
- I need Scripture to correct me.
- I need wise people around me.
- I will repent quickly instead of defending sin.

## Practice

Before making a major decision, ask:

1. Am I refusing correction?
2. Am I exaggerating my strength?
3. Am I hiding anything?
4. Am I more eager to be right than to be holy?
5. Have I prayed with surrender, not just with preference?
