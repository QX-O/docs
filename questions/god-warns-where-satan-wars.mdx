## The extracted question

The notes say, “Wherever Satan is warring, God will warn,” and connect this with studying the Bible correctly.

## Short answer

God warns through Scripture, conviction, wisdom, circumstances, and faithful people. His warnings lead toward truth, repentance, protection, and deeper trust. Satan’s warfare leads toward fear, confusion, isolation, pride, and despair.

## How God warns

### Scripture

The clearest warnings are already written. If an impression contradicts Scripture, it should be rejected.

### Conviction

The Spirit may press on the conscience about sin, danger, or disobedience. Conviction is specific enough to respond to and hopeful enough to return to God.

### Wise counsel

God often uses mature believers to see what we are missing. Isolation makes deception easier.

### Patterns and fruit

Repeated chaos, hidden compromise, bitterness, or fear may reveal an area that needs attention.

## Difference between warning and fear

| God’s warning | Satanic fear |
| --- | --- |
| Calls you to truth | Pulls you into confusion |
| Leads to prayer | Leads to panic |
| Invites repentance | Produces despair |
| Can be tested by Scripture | Resists testing |
| Often brings sober peace | Produces torment |

## Studying the Bible correctly

To study carefully:

1. Read the passage in context.
2. Notice who is speaking and who is being addressed.
3. Compare unclear impressions with clear Scripture.
4. Avoid using isolated verses to justify fear or selfish desire.
5. Let Scripture correct your assumptions.
