## The extracted question

The notes describe a “law of knowing”: you cannot unknow something once you know it.

## Short answer

Once truth is revealed, responsibility increases. Knowing should lead to obedience, not avoidance. The point is not to live crushed by guilt but to respond faithfully to the light God has given.

## What knowing changes

### It removes the excuse of ignorance

Before someone understands, they may act blindly. After truth is revealed, pretending not to know becomes a form of resistance.

### It calls for obedience

Biblical knowledge is not meant to remain information. It should become trust, repentance, worship, and action.

### It deepens accountability

Greater light brings greater responsibility. This is why humility matters: knowledge without surrender can become pride.

## What to do when you know

1. Thank God for revealing truth.
2. Ask what obedience looks like now.
3. Take the next faithful step instead of waiting for perfect understanding.
4. Confess where you have delayed.
5. Share wisely if the truth could help someone else.

## What not to do with knowledge

- Do not use knowledge to feel superior.
- Do not delay obedience by demanding more signs.
- Do not weaponize truth without love.
- Do not confuse information with transformation.
