## The extracted question

The notes ask: if the death of a perfect man would reverse the tragedy caused by sin, why would Satan not delay the death of Christ?

## Short answer

The cross was hidden wisdom. Satan and sinful powers treated Jesus’ death as a victory, but God used that very act to defeat sin, death, and the devil. The enemy’s hatred, pride, and blindness made him serve the plan he opposed.

## Key idea: the cross looked like defeat

At the surface level, the crucifixion looked like the silencing of Jesus. Religious leaders rejected him, Rome executed him, the disciples scattered, and Jesus died publicly in shame. From a merely earthly view, the mission appeared over.

But the gospel reveals the opposite: the cross was the place where Jesus bore sin, exposed evil, disarmed spiritual powers, and opened the way to resurrection life.

## Why Satan may not have delayed it

### 1. Pride is blinding

The notes say pride comes before a fall because it is blinding. Pride does not merely make someone arrogant; it distorts perception. If Satan’s aim was to oppose God by destroying the Son, pride could blind him to the deeper wisdom of God.

### 2. Hatred can become self-defeating

The notes suggest that Satan may hate humanity so much that hatred blinded him. Evil often consumes itself. A destructive will may focus so intensely on immediate damage that it cannot perceive the redemptive purpose God is working through suffering.

### 3. The wisdom of God was not obvious to worldly powers

Paul says that if the rulers of this age had understood God’s wisdom, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. The cross was not a backup plan; it was God’s wisdom operating in a way evil did not master.

### 4. Satan can exploit the moment while still losing the war

The notes also raise the possibility that Satan used the situation to condemn as many individuals as possible. That fits a broader pattern: even when Satan cannot stop God’s plan, he tries to maximize fear, accusation, betrayal, confusion, and unbelief around it.

## What the cross accomplished

- Jesus bore sin as the spotless sacrifice.
- Jesus revealed the love and justice of God.
- Jesus defeated the accusing power of the enemy.
- Jesus opened reconciliation between God and humanity.
- Jesus turned public shame into resurrection glory.

## Practical application

Do not judge God’s victory by how a moment looks. The cross teaches that the darkest moment can be the place where God is doing his deepest work.
