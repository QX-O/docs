## The extracted question

The notes say Christ knows and cares about your good works, and even more about your posture.

## Short answer

Christ sees both what you do and why you do it. Good works matter, but heart posture matters because works can be done from love, faith, pride, fear, performance, or a desire to be seen.

## Why good works matter

Good works are not the root of salvation, but they are fruit of living faith. They bless people, glorify God, and make obedience visible.

## Why posture matters

The same outward action can come from different inward places.

| Work | Possible posture |
| --- | --- |
| Giving | Love, pressure, pride, or control |
| Serving | Worship, exhaustion, resentment, or attention-seeking |
| Fasting | Humility, manipulation, performance, or hunger for God |
| Correction | Love, anger, superiority, or protection |

## Questions for examining posture

Ask yourself:

1. Am I doing this for God or for image?
2. Would I still obey if no one noticed?
3. Is this producing love or resentment?
4. Am I trying to earn what Christ gives by grace?
5. Does this action align with Scripture and the character of Jesus?

## A healthy posture

A healthy posture is humble, honest, surrendered, loving, and dependent on grace. It does not despise good works; it purifies them.

## Final connection

This page closes the study because the goal is not merely to understand Satan’s tactics. The goal is to belong fully to Christ, resist evil, walk in truth, and obey with a heart that loves God.
